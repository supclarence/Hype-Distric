Who We Are

Hype District is a Filipino streetwear brand created by passionate creators who believe in style, culture, and identity. We build pieces that represent real street energy while staying affordable and high quality.

Vision

To become one of the top streetwear brands in the Philippines and compete globally with originality and authenticity.

Mission

• Compete internationally
• Support local creatives
